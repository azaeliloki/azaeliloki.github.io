West Africa

One language corridor, one translator

ECOWAS (Economic Community of West African States) trade runs across a language border: Ghana and Nigeria work in English, while Togo, Benin, Côte d’Ivoire and Senegal work in French. Every contract, tender and compliance file that crosses that border needs professional French–English translation — exactly the pair I am sworn for. I work remotely for companies and NGOs across the region, with delivery by email and invoicing in FCFA, EUR or USD.

Operational Policies

Terms for companies and NGOs

These terms apply to all transactions without exception.

All monthly Circles are paid in advance. Invoices are due by the 5th of each month, before that month’s sessions begin. Accepted: bank transfer, Ecobank Mobile, Moov Money Flooz, Mixx.

A minimum of 24 hours’ notice is required to reschedule any session. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice are permanently forfeited and billed at full cost.

All sessions allocated within a monthly Circle must be used within that calendar month. Unused sessions do not roll over under any circumstances.

Institutional accounts and NGOs operate via formal pro forma documentation. Full payment or an authorised purchase order is required before work begins, followed by a standard tax invoice on delivery.
