West Africa

One language corridor, one translator

ECOWAS (Economic Community of West African States) trade runs across a language border: Ghana and Nigeria work in English, while Togo, Benin, Côte d’Ivoire and Burkina Faso work in French. Every contract, tender and compliance file that crosses that border needs professional FR↔EN translation — exactly the pair I am sworn for. I work remotely for companies and NGOs across the region, with delivery by email and invoicing in FCFA, EUR or USD.

Areas served: Lomé, Togo (on site) · West Africa & worldwide, remotely.

Operational Policies

Clear terms. No surprises.

These terms apply to all transactions without exception.

All monthly subscriptions operate under an advance payment model. Invoices are strictly due and payable by the 5th calendar day of the month before sessions begin. Accepted: Bank Transfer, Ecobank Mobile, Moov Money, Mixx.

A minimum of 24 hours’ notice is required to reschedule any session. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice are permanently forfeited and billed at full cost.

All sessions allocated within a monthly subscription must be consumed within that calendar month. Unused sessions do not roll over under any circumstances.

Institutional accounts and NGOs operate via formal pro forma documentation. Full payment or an authorised purchase order is required before work begins, followed by a standard fiscal invoice upon delivery.
