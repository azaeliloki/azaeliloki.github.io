Services

Types of interpretation offered

Whether you’re negotiating a contract, welcoming an international delegation or running a seminar, I offer tailored interpretation services built on absolute fidelity to the message and complete confidentiality.

  • Liaison interpretationbusiness meetings, site visits, professional negotiations, as well as personal appointments: consular or immigration interviews, notary, bank, medical or administrative appointments.
  • Consecutive interpretationofficial speeches, press conferences, short presentations (the speaker pauses every few sentences).
  • Official support / diplomatic missionsadministrative procedures, contract signings, official visits in Lomé.
  • Simultaneous conference interpretationfor large multilingual conferences and seminars, delivered by a two-interpreter relay team (booth/headsets). Custom quote only.

Rates

Rates — liaison, consecutive & official support

Rates shown are excl. tax, applicable to the three services above. If a formal invoice is requested (individuals or businesses), VAT is added at the applicable rate. Simultaneous conference interpretation (booth, two-interpreter team) is always quoted individually, at a higher rate. International and institutional clients can be invoiced in EUR, USD or FCFA; payment by bank transfer or international wire (SWIFT).

Liaison or booth? Up to about a dozen participants — a meeting, a negotiation, a site visit — liaison interpreting is enough, with one interpreter. Beyond that, or as soon as there is a platform, an audience or a full-day session, the assignment moves to booth interpreting: two interpreters per language relaying every twenty to thirty minutes, plus the equipment.

Half-day (up to 4h)150,000 FCFA (excl. tax)
Full day (up to 8h)250,000 FCFA (excl. tax)
Additional hour40,000 FCFA (excl. tax) / hour

Rates do not include travel or accommodation costs if the event takes place outside Lomé. For extended assignments or online interpretation (Zoom/Teams), contact me for a custom quote.
