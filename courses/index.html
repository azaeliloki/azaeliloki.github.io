Yes. Online Private Classes are available worldwide via Zoom. Students and professionals from any country in West Africa or internationally can enrol. Sessions are scheduled flexibly Mon–Fri 8:00–20:00 and Sat 8:00–18:00 (GMT+0). Payment is accepted via bank transfer and international wire.

Before your first session, you complete a short placement test (1h) that locates your exact level on the CEFR scale from A1 to C2. It requires no preparation — just a conversation and a few written exercises. The results allow me to design a learning plan that starts at exactly the right level, so no time is wasted on content you already know.
