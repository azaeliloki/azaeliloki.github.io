Volume discounts apply to complete multi-document files from five pages. A birth certificate, diploma or police clearance is billed per page; contracts and procedural documents are billed per word.

What affects the price

  • Document type — standard civil-status and diploma pages are billed per page; technical, legal and long documents per word.
  • Urgency — express 24h delivery adds 50%; urgent business work adds 30–50%.
  • Volume — files from five pages qualify for a volume discount, quoted individually.
  • Certification — a stamped, signed sworn copy is included in the certified page rate; extra certified prints are billed per copy.
