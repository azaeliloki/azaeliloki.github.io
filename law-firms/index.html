West Africa

Cross-border files in the ECOWAS corridor

Counterparties and courts in Ghana and Nigeria work in English; Togo, Benin, Côte d’Ivoire and Burkina Faso work in French. When a file crosses that line — a contract governed by Ghanaian law, a judgment to be produced in Lomé, a power of attorney signed in Lagos — it needs professional FR↔EN legal translation, the exact pair I am sworn for. I work remotely for firms across the region.

How cross-border files work across West Africa →

Acceptance of a Lomé sworn translation always remains the receiving authority’s decision — tell me the destination and I will match the format it requires. Areas served: Lomé, Togo (on site) · West Africa & worldwide, remotely.

The Principle

A document drafted in English has no effect in Togo unless accompanied by a translation bearing the stamp and signature of a sworn translator accredited by the Cour d’appel de Lomé. That translation engages my professional liability just as the document engages yours.

Framework

Professional secrecy and billing options

Confidentiality

Professional secrecy without reservation. Documents deleted thirty days after delivery; only the register of certified translations is kept indefinitely, and it holds no document content. NDA signed on request.

Billing

Two options: invoice the firm, or invoice the end client with the firm as referrer. Quote, pro forma, purchase order and final invoice. Firms outside Togo can be invoiced in EUR, USD or FCFA and settle by bank transfer or international wire (SWIFT).

Practice

Worked examples

Succession with a foreign will — a notary opens an estate in Lomé that includes a will drafted in English abroad. I produce a sworn FR rendering of the will and any accompanying probate extracts so the notarial deed can cite them with legal effect. Turnaround is usually quoted by page count once the scans are complete.

Power of attorney for a notary — a client abroad signs an English power of attorney that must be used before a Togolese notary. I deliver a sworn EN→FR translation with stamp and signature so the notary can annex it to the acte without delaying the signing appointment.

Foreign judgment for exequatur — counsel needs an English (or French) court decision recognised in Togo. I translate the judgment and operative parts as a sworn certified copy; the firm handles the exequatur procedure itself — my role stops at a translation the court can rely on.

Delivery

Getting the document to you

  • Hand delivery in Lomé. Stamped paper copy, by appointment or delivered to your chambers.
  • Courier anywhere in Togo. Stamped paper copy to the address of your choice. Fees at cost, stated in the quote. Allow 24 to 72 hours in transit depending on destination.
  • Secure PDF — included. For electronic filing and internal circulation, delivered on completion.

Tell me the hearing or filing date when you send the file and the schedule is worked back from it. For a recorded statement or hearing, see transcription and translation of recordings.
